Before You Start

A Few Things Worth Knowing

Is this medical advice?

No. This is training education, not a substitute for a doctor or physical therapist. If you have an existing injury, are pregnant, or are returning from injury, get cleared before starting, and stop any exercise that produces sharp pain.

Do I need a gym?

No — every protocol has a Bodyweight Edition requiring only a pull-up bar, a resistance band, and a bench. Prefer to lift? The Gym Edition uses real loaded strength work instead.

What if I'm just starting out?

The method meets you at your level and scales intensity, not standards — beginners get the same precision as advanced athletes, dosed appropriately.
